Other ways to help

Money is the flexible one

Shelters can stretch a dollar further than almost anyone. A donation covers whatever is actually short that week — vaccines, a surgery, a heat pump in July — rather than another bag of the wrong food.

Foster if you have a spare room

Foster homes are the real bottleneck. Every dog in a spare bedroom is a kennel freed for the next one, and dogs show their true personality in a house in a way they never do in a run.

Volunteer an afternoon

Walking, laundry, transport runs, photographing dogs so their listings do not look like mugshots. Unglamorous, and it is what keeps the doors open.

Grow something and share it

Plenty of shelters run fundraiser plant sales in spring. A flat of seedlings started from a packet of our zinnias costs you a windowsill and turns into real money for them.

Where our part ends

We are a seed company, not a charity, and we want to be straight about the difference. We do not collect donations, add a round-up at checkout, or claim that a portion of your purchase goes anywhere but back into growing seed. What we do is put the link above in front of every person who visits, because that costs you nothing and it works.

Want us to feature a different rescue? Tell us about the one you support — we rotate who is listed here.
